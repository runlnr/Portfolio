Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how I handle personal data, the compliance standards I follow, and the data protection measures in place on this website. I'm committed to transparency, data minimization, and honoring the privacy rights of every visitor and prospective collaborator.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Data Controller & Contact Details

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data collected through this website is:

Name: Nam Pham

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Direct Privacy Inquiries: work@scherre.com

Information I Collect

I adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. I only collect information that is strictly necessary to evaluate project inquiries and maintain technical reliability:

  • Voluntary Project Inquiries: When you submit an inquiry through my contact form, I collect your name or company identifier, email address, selected discipline of interest, estimated budget range, and your project brief message.
  • Automated Server Logs: Standard automated HTTP request headers (including browser user-agent, operating system, requested page URL, and access timestamps) are processed transiently to ensure reliable routing, security defense, and server health.

Legal Basis for Processing

I process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: For project inquiries submitted voluntarily through the contact form.
  • Legitimate Interest: For automated server logs and aggregate site analytics, to maintain the security, integrity, and technical reliability of the website and to understand aggregate visitor engagement.
  • Pre-contractual Steps: Where you contact me regarding a potential project, to take steps at your request prior to entering into a service agreement.

Data Retention

Project inquiry data (contact details and brief messages) is retained for up to 24 months from the date of your last communication with me, or until you request erasure, whichever comes first. Automated server logs are retained transiently and are not kept longer than 90 days except where required for security investigation.

Cookies & Web Analytics

This website does not use cookies of any kind — no advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, and no analytics cookies.

To understand aggregate site traffic and performance, I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first analytics service. It collects only anonymized, aggregate data (page views and Core Web Vitals performance metrics) via a lightweight script. It does not set cookies, does not fingerprint your device, does not track you across visits, and does not build any individual visitor profile. Because no personal data or device storage is involved, no cookie consent banner is required for this service.

Cookies & Local Storage

Separate from cookies, this website uses client-side sessionStorage and localStorage for functional UI state management. These are not cookies, are not sent to any server, and do not require consent, but are disclosed here for transparency:

  • Session Intro State: Tracks whether the initial entry animation has already played during your browsing session to prevent repetitive interruptions.
  • Smooth Page Navigation: Manages the seamless cross-fade page transition curtain between case studies.

This website does not deploy third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking trackers.

Legal Compliance & Regulatory Framework

My data protection practices are governed by and comply with:

  • Decree No. 13/2023/NĐ-CP (Vietnam): Full compliance with the Personal Data Protection Decree of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam concerning legitimate data collection, user consent, and storage protection.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Alignment with international standards on lawful processing, data portability, and user sovereignty for international visitors.

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under applicable data protection laws, you retain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a verified copy of all project inquiry records associated with your email address.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections or updates to any previously submitted contact details.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You may request the immediate, permanent deletion of your inquiry data and email communication logs from my records.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that I limit how your data is used while a rectification or objection request is being resolved.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interest, including aggregate analytics.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority — the Ministry of Public Security in Vietnam, or your local data protection authority if you are located in the EEA.

To exercise any of these rights, email me at work@scherre.com. I will fulfill verified requests within 7 business days without charge.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at individuals under the age of 16, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided me with personal data, please contact me so I can promptly delete it.
