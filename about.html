Nam Pham is a designer, ex-competitive programmer, self-declared table tennis pro, and lifelong Kanye West fan. He was born in Dak Lak, Vietnam, and moved with his family to Ho Chi Minh City in third grade, where he grew up. He studied computer science at VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted, where working with code and minimal IDEs shaped his taste in design. He is now on a gap year before heading to Taiwan in a few months for a bachelor's degree in either energy engineering or interaction design.

He works remotely as a freelancer, and is currently under contract with Seros Partners, producing their social media graphics. He spent years teaching himself graphic design (currently on year 3), building case studies the whole time, and that work has taken him to clients in Mexico, Germany, the UAE, Spain, the United States, and Vietnam. His work spans visual identity, brand systems, sports graphics, websites, and editorial.
